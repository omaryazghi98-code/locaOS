# Field Work & Funding Plan — Morocco

## Core rule

Field validation and product development run in parallel. Funding does not become a prerequisite for validation, and funding is not a reason to invent product demand or jobs.

## Phase 0 — $0 validation

### Field work
- Shadow 5–10 Moroccan rental agencies, prioritizing medium/high operational maturity.
- Include a small Tetouan comparison group rather than assuming the local market represents the whole ICP.
- Observe actual workflows: reservations, assignment, contracts, inspections, deposits, payments, returns, damage, maintenance, WhatsApp/phone, spreadsheets and paper.
- Record repeated pain, operational risk, time loss, financial leakage and workarounds.
- Ask what currently breaks, what consumes staff time, what creates stress and what causes lost revenue.
- Do not lead interviews with the NAVI solution; validate the problem first.

### Funding work
- Build a Morocco funding map in parallel.
- Track eligibility, stage, amount, instrument, own contribution, eligible expenses, job requirements, deadlines and application status.
- Distinguish grant/subsidy, honour loan, bank credit guarantee, regional support, incubator support and equity.

## Phase 1 — Evidence + prototype

Translate observed problems into concrete workflows and test prototypes against the real observations.

Measure:
- time saved
- error reduction
- adoption friction
- operational visibility
- willingness to pay

Do not optimize for cosmetic feature requests.

## Phase 2 — MVP + pilot

Run a real pilot with one or a few agencies.

Measure:
- real transactions processed
- active usage
- repeat usage
- workflow completion
- errors avoided
- time saved
- customer/staff feedback
- willingness to pay
- onboarding/support burden

The goal is commercial and operational proof, not vanity metrics.

## Phase 3 — Early funding

Once there is evidence, apply selectively to programs for which the company/project is actually eligible.

Potential channels to verify at application time:
- FORSA and successor/current calls
- CRI Tanger-Tétouan-Al Hoceima and regional programs
- Technopark/incubators
- innovation and entrepreneurship programs
- relevant international/EU programs

Never describe an old or closed call as currently open. Verify every deadline and eligibility rule before applying.

## Phase 4 — Company + credit

After legal setup, MVP and credible pilots, evaluate instruments such as Tamwilcom/Damane Intelak and regional financing.

Debt is appropriate only where repayment is credible. A guarantee is not a grant.

## Phase 5 — Investment + scale

Use equity/strategic capital when acceleration is justified:
- senior engineering
- product
- sales
- customer success
- infrastructure
- integrations
- WhatsApp/telephony
- public agency websites
- geographic expansion

Hire field sales/agency-success staff when the founder-led sales motion is proven and the workload justifies it. Do not manufacture jobs to satisfy an assumed funding quota.

## Business-plan evidence pack

Maintain:
- ICP definition
- observed pain points and evidence
- TAM/SAM/SOM assumptions
- pricing hypothesis
- ARPU and gross-margin assumptions
- CAC and sales-cycle assumptions
- onboarding/support cost
- churn/retention assumptions
- break-even scenario
- pilot results
- funding requirement and use of funds
- realistic hiring plan
- expansion scenarios

## Decision gates

**Gate A:** Is the problem repeated and expensive enough to solve?

**Gate B:** Does the workflow actually improve with locaOS?

**Gate C:** Will an agency use it repeatedly?

**Gate D:** Will an agency pay for it?

**Gate E:** Is external capital now the constraint rather than product uncertainty?

Only move to the next funding/hiring intensity after the relevant evidence exists.
